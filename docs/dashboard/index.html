Currency & methodology

Rates mean NZD per one unit of source currency. Non-NZD rates start unset. Enter your own documented positive rates; they apply to every year as a fixed-rate scenario.

Read before interpreting
  • Rates and this note are stored only in this browser, not written to the public site or shared with other visitors.
  • Profit assumes unit costs use each order's currency. It excludes any unrecorded freight, tax or overhead.
  • The threshold is an analytical definition, not a verified delivery SLA.
  • Customer and product financial ranks follow your current currency and filters.
  • 2019 orders end on 12 December. Shipments extend through 28 December.
  • Product 20 is retained despite no recorded sales. Maps use supplied coordinates; basemap tiles require internet.
  • This public static site has no authentication: its bundled records are downloadable.
